Hello! I'm a mechanical engineer turned data science enthusiast with a strong foundation in mechanical design, thermal engineering, and quality & reliability engineering.

My career began in mechanical design and evolved into a thermal engineering specialty. Along the way, I found myself increasingly drawn to data analysis and automation to improve engineering outcomes. That interest eventually led me to self-train in scripting and data science methods.

Currently, I work as a Quality & Reliability Engineer in the silicon manufacturing industry, where I apply statistical analysis, root cause investigations, and predictive techniques to ensure product performance. It's a role that sits at the intersection of engineering and data—and it’s confirmed for me that data science is where I want to grow next.

To formalize my transition, I’m now pursuing a Master’s in Data Science, aiming to expand my skills in machine learning, data modeling, data engineering, and advanced analytics.

My ideal position would bridge both worlds: using my mechanical and thermal systems expertise to work closely with data and software engineering teams. I’m especially passionate about acting as a technical liaison—translating real-world engineering challenges into data science problems and solutions.
